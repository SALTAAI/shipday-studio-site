The composition

Built around one
precious root.

Iris rhizomes rest for three years before they give up their scent. We let the formula take its time too, so each note unfolds in order rather than all at once.

TopBergamot, pink pepperThe first ten minutes, bright and dry
HeartOrris butter, violet leafPowdery, cool, a little like suede
BaseAmbrette, white cedarA soft skin scent that lasts into the evening
